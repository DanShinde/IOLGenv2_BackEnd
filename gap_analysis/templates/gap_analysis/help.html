Who can do what

Every page requires logging in. Beyond that, what you can do depends on your account:

Account type Can view Can do
Staff Everything Everything: create/edit/delete employees, roles, skills, benchmarks, bulk update, approve or override any self-rating, create development plans.
Manager Everything Approve or override their own direct reports' self-ratings and edit their skill levels. Everything else is read-only. See Self vs. manager rating for how the manager link is set up.
Any other user Everything Read-only everywhere, plus submitting their own self-ratings on My Skills if their account is linked to an employee record.

"Staff" is the is_staff flag on a Django account (set via createsuperuser or Django admin). A staff badge next to your name in the top-right of every page confirms it.

Dashboard

The homepage. Organized into four tabs so the one number that matters most loads first.

Overview tab

A large "Org-wide Skills Met %" tile (colored green/amber/red by how healthy it is) next to three supporting numbers: total people tracked, skills tracked, and average gap score. If any employee has a skill gap greater than 2 levels, a red "Critical Gaps" table appears right below, naming who and which skills. Quick Actions at the bottom jump straight to adding an employee, role, or skill record (staff only) or viewing the employee list.

Charts tab

Team Overview: a bar chart comparing the average actual level vs. the average benchmark required level, per skill, across everyone. Role Analysis: the same comparison, but filtered to one role at a time — pick a role from the dropdown and click Filter.

Insights tab

Role Gap Summary (average gap per role), Top Skill Gaps (employees with the most total gap), Top Performers (employees exceeding their benchmarks most often), and Skills Needing Training (skills with the highest average gap org-wide).

Distribution tab

Benchmark Compliance: how many employees are fully meeting their benchmarks, partially meeting them, or need training, shown as a stacked bar. Skill Distribution: how many skills fall into each category (Technical, Soft Skills, Leadership, etc).

A skill counts double toward every gap score on this page if it's flagged Mandatory on its role benchmark — a missed must-have skill moves the numbers more than a missed nice-to-have.

People menu

The full list of employees tracked in the system. Filter by designation or status. Each row shows total skills, average gap, skills-met %, and a Training Req. button — click it to open a popup listing exactly which skills that person still needs to reach benchmark, instead of cramming them into the row. Staff can add, edit, or delete employees (including linking them to a login and a manager — see below); everyone can export the current filtered list to CSV.

The list of job roles/designations. Click a role's "Manage Benchmark" icon to open its required-skills page, where Staff can add or remove which skills that role requires, at what level (0–5), and whether each one is Mandatory (counts double in gap scoring) or optional.

Set one skill to one level for many employees at once — useful right after onboarding a whole team. Tick the employees, pick the skill, click the target level (0–5), submit.

Skills menu
Skill Search — "Find Experts by Skill"

Pick any skill from the dropdown to see everyone who's been rated on it, ranked from the highest level down — the top scorer gets a trophy badge. Useful for finding who to ask for help, or who could mentor others, on a specific skill.

My Skills — self-service rating

Your own view of the skills your role requires. Enter how you'd rate yourself (0–5) per skill; it's saved as Pending Review until your manager or staff approves or corrects it. This page only works if your login is linked to an employee record — see Self vs. manager rating.

Manage Skills & Benchmarks Staff only

The master list of every skill that exists in the system (name, category, description) and the raw list of every role–skill benchmark. This is where new skills get created before they can be attached to a role.

Plans menu
Development Plans

Every gap-closing action recorded across the whole organization, in one filterable list — who it's for, which skill, the action (e.g. "Complete Django REST course"), an optional resource link, a target date (flagged red if overdue), and status (Not Started / In Progress / Completed / Cancelled). Staff create, edit, and delete plans, usually from an employee's own profile.

Pending Reviews

Every self-rating awaiting a decision. Staff see every pending rating org-wide; a Manager sees only their own direct reports'. Click through to the employee's profile to approve or override.

Employee profile

Click any employee's name (from Skill Matrix, Skill Search, or a dashboard table) to open their profile. It has four tabs:

Skills Assessment

One row per required skill: Required level, the employee's Self Rating (with its review-status badge and an Approve button when pending), the official Manager Level (editable inline by Staff or that person's Manager), the Gap, and a Met/Warning/Gap status badge. Editing the Manager Level updates the gap and status instantly, without a page reload.

Visualization

A radar chart plotting actual level against required level across every skill at once — the fastest way to see someone's overall shape: where they're strong (inside the red dashed line) and where they're short (outside it).

History

One small trend card per skill, showing every recorded level change over time with the date. A skill only ever rated once shows a single point labeled "First recorded"; a skill with no rating at all yet is labeled "Not yet assessed."

Development Plans

Every action plan created for this specific person. Staff can add a new one directly from here, pre-scoped to this employee's own required/assessed skills.

The Skill Card button opens a printable one-page summary of the same data — handy for a performance review conversation or a PDF export (Print → Save as PDF).

Self vs. manager rating — how it fits together

This is the most connected feature in the app, so here's the full loop end to end:

  1. Link the employee to a login. Staff open the employee's Edit page (People → Skill Matrix → Edit) and set Linked Login to their Django user account. Without this, that person can't use My Skills at all.
  2. Link the employee to their manager. On the same Edit page, set the Manager field to the employee's manager — who must themselves already exist as an employee record with their own Linked Login. This is the step that's easiest to miss, and skipping it is the single most common reason an "Approve" click never seems to complete: without this link, the system can't confirm the person clicking Approve is actually this employee's manager, so the request is silently rejected every time.
  3. Employee submits a self-rating. They go to Skills → My Skills, enter a level per skill. It's saved immediately as Pending Review.
  4. Manager or staff reviews it. Either from Plans → Pending Reviews, or directly on the employee's profile (Skills Assessment tab), they see the self-rating next to the current official level.
  5. Approve or override. Clicking Approve accepts the self-rating as-is and copies it into the official Manager Level, marking it Approved. Typing a different number directly into the Manager Level field instead marks it Overridden — the self-rating stays visible for reference, but the manager's number is what counts toward every gap score.

Only the approved/overridden Manager Level ever feeds into gap scores, the dashboard, or skill history — a pending self-rating is informational only until someone acts on it.

Tips & FAQ

Almost always a missing Manager link. Ask staff to open your employee record and confirm the Manager field points to you, and that your own employee record has your login set under Linked Login. See Self vs. manager rating above.

Skills Met % is a simple count — what fraction of skills have zero gap. Avg Gap Score is weighted: a missed Mandatory skill counts twice as much as a missed optional one, so it can move even when the raw met-percentage doesn't.

No — every delete confirmation page says so before you commit, and it's final. Deleting an employee also removes their skill records, history, and development plans.

Creating, editing, and deleting most records is staff-only. If you manage people, you can still edit your own direct reports' skill levels and approve their self-ratings — everything else is read-only for non-staff accounts by design.
